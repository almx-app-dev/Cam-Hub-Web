One less account. More control.

Bring video from different sources into one clear view.

Add an individual camera or connect selected compatible NVR, DVR, and NAS surveillance systems. Cam Hub organizes their channels in one consistent interface for faster viewing and switching.

01

Direct connection

Connect with the address and access information already configured on your equipment, without registering a vendor cloud account just to view video.

02

LiveView Mix

Combine channels from different cameras and recorders into your own monitoring dashboard.

03

Open protocols first

Supports ONVIF and RTSP streaming, with compatible connections for selected equipment.

04

Live and playback

View live video and search or play recordings when the equipment supports those capabilities.

05

Monitoring on Apple TV

Use LiveView Mix, equipment walls, and full-screen video on Apple TV. Create an encrypted Apple TV backup on iPhone or iPad, then restore it without a PIN.

06

Webhook control panels

Turn a LiveView Mix blank slot into HTTP webhook buttons for access, lighting, scenes, or other services you manage. Sensitive actions can require confirmation.
