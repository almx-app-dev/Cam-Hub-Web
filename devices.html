Cam Hub on each device

Cam Hub runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and an Apple Watch app fires the buttons of the control panels. The tables show what each device offers, and how iPhone Duo uses its displays when it is closed, open or half folded.

  • One subscription covers every iPhone, iPad and Apple TV that uses the same Apple Account.

iPhone Duo: closed, open and half folded

On iPhone Duo, Cam Hub uses the outer display while the phone is closed and the larger inner display while it is open. The screens lay themselves out again whenever the phone is folded, unfolded or turned.

How the phone is heldWhat Cam Hub shows
Closed (outer display)The same screens as on other iPhones.
Open flat (inner display)The camera walls of the Live tab and of the device pages show two columns, as on an iPad. The full-screen LiveView Mix wall fills the whole inner display. A camera can open in a window of its own with Open in New Window, so that one window keeps a wall and another the camera with its recordings.
Half folded, hinge across the screen (standing like a small laptop)The camera page shows the picture above the fold and the controls, the recordings and the PTZ pad below it. With Two canvases switched on in Settings, LiveView Mix is cut at the hinge into an upper and a lower canvas, each with its own cameras and control panels.
Half folded, held upright like a bookFor a camera with recording playback, the camera page shows the live picture on one half and the same camera at the same time of day yesterday on the other. The device page shows its camera wall on one half and the camera that was tapped on the other.

The Two canvases switch appears only on iPhone Duo. Opened flat or closed, the phone shows one LiveView Mix wall again.
