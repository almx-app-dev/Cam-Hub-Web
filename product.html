CAM HUB PRODUCT

從加入設備，到建立自己的 LiveView Mix 牆。

Cam Hub 將設備連接、通道整理、即時監看與錄影回放集中在清楚一致的流程中。

閱讀操作手冊

01

Add your equipment

Enter the address and credentials for equipment you own or are authorized to access. Cam Hub can identify selected compatible devices or connect through ONVIF.

02

Choose the channels

Review the cameras exposed by a camera or recorder and enable the channels you want to view.

03

Build LiveView Mix

Combine channels from different cameras and recorders into one monitoring dashboard.

04

View live and recorded video

Open a channel for live viewing. Recording search and playback are available when supported by the equipment.

05

Plans and limits

Cam Hub includes a free allowance. Camera plans and NVR plans each raise a different limit.

Plans and what each one unlocks
PlanBillingWhat it unlocks
FreeNoneAt most 2 enabled channels in the whole app (standalone cameras and recorder channels count together) and at most 1 recorder.
Camera plansYearlyRaise the number of standalone cameras you can enable to 8 (Cam Home), 16 (Cam Plus) or unlimited (Cam Max).
NVR plansMonthlyRaise the number of recorders to 2 (NVR Starter), 4 (NVR Pro) or unlimited (NVR Max), and every channel of those recorders can be used.
05.01

Two independent kinds

Camera plans and NVR plans are independent and can be combined. A camera plan does not raise the recorder allowance, and an NVR plan does not raise the standalone-camera limit. Prices are shown in the app.

05.02

Example

With Cam Plus and NVR Pro: 4 recorders with all their channels and up to 16 standalone cameras.
