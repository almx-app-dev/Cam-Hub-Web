13Settings

The Settings tab holds the plan, the default quality and picture settings, the choices for LiveView Mix and its pages and for the start of the app, the backup and sharing of cameras, the language, the reset actions, the legal pages, the version and the link to this manual. This chapter lists every row in screen order with its effect and its default, and points to the chapter that explains it in depth.

The sections of the Settings tab

Path: Settings tab at the bottom of the screen. The tab is a single list that scrolls. The sections follow each other in the order of the table.

  1. Tap the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen.

    Check: The screen shows the settings, beginning with the section Subscription.

  2. Scroll down to the end of the list.

    Check: The sections follow in the order of the table. The last sections are Reset, Legal and About, followed by the row User Manual.

A short text below most sections explains the section. Changes to switches and selectors apply at once; no row needs a confirmation, except the three reset actions. Apple TV has its own settings screen; see Chapter 14, “Apple TV”.

Subscription

RowWhat it does
PlansOpens the plans page over the settings. The grey line below the name shows the current plan, or Free. When a camera plan and an NVR plan are held, the two names are joined with a plus sign. As long as the names of the plans cannot be loaded, the line shows Camera plan or NVR plan.
Manage SubscriptionOpens the system page for subscriptions. There a plan can be changed or canceled, and the renewal date is shown.
Restore PurchasesSynchronizes the purchases of the Apple Account with the app and shows the result.

The plans, the subscription steps and the redemption of a code are described in “Subscribing, restoring purchases and redeeming a code”. The steps below show the page and the restoring of purchases.

  1. In the section Subscription, tap Plans.

    Check: The page Plans opens over the settings. It lists the camera plans and the NVR plans and marks the current plan.

  2. Tap Done in the top-right corner.

    Check: The settings list is shown again.

  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

    Check: After a moment, an alert states the result: Purchases restored., No purchases to restore. or Restore failed. Please try again. When the system sign-in screen is canceled, no alert appears.

  4. Tap OK.

    Check: The alert closes. When purchases were found, the allowance of the plan is updated.

Default quality

The two rows set whether a picture starts as SD (lighter) or HD (sharper). The text below the section advises that viewing one camera can use more resources for a sharper picture, that the capacity of the device matters when many cameras are viewed at once, and that SD and HD look almost the same when each tile is small.

RowUsed byDefault
Single cameraThe single-camera view, see “Snapshot, sound and quality”, and the close-up of a camera in a wall, see “The full-screen wall of a device”.HD
Multiple camerasThe Live tab and the Full Screen Wall, see “SD and HD on a wall”, and the thumbnails of the device page, see “The device page”.SD

LiveView Mix walls are not affected: each camera slot has its own quality; see “Choosing cameras and quality”. For all screens, see “SD and HD on device screens”.

  1. In the Settings tab, find the section Default quality.

    Check: Two rows show a selector with SD and HD. Without a change, HD is selected for Single camera and SD for Multiple cameras.

  2. In the row Multiple cameras, tap HD.

    Check: HD is selected. The thumbnails of the device page change to HD at once. The Live tab changes to HD at once as well, unless the quality button of the Live tab has been used since the app started.

  3. To return, tap SD in the same row.

    Check: SD is selected.

The quality can still be switched while viewing; that change is not stored, as the text below the section states.

Picture: black bars and edge-to-edge

Both rows decide how much of the screen the picture uses: one inside its tile, the other against the edges of the device.

  • Black bars (default Default): the starting mode for the handling of black bars. Default shows the whole picture with black bars where the shapes differ, Enlarge to fill enlarges the picture in proportion and cuts off its edges, and Stretch to fill stretches the picture and can distort it; see “The device page”. The setting is the starting mode of the device page, the Full Screen Wall, the single-camera view and the Live tab. The device page and the Full Screen Wall read it each time they open, the single-camera view applies it at once, and the Live tab reads it when the app starts. The change of mode on a screen is not stored. LiveView Mix has its own setting for each wall; see “Black bars, edge-to-edge video and the angle of the device”.
  • Edge-to-edge video (default on): while the switch is on, full-screen video reaches into the area of the Dynamic Island and the rounded corners. While it is off, the picture stays clear of them, with black bars. The switch applies to the Full Screen Wall, to the full-screen wall of LiveView Mix and to the single-camera view held sideways; see “Black bars, rotation and landscape”.

  1. In the section Picture, tap Black bars.

    Check: A menu lists Default, Enlarge to fill and Stretch to fill. The current choice is marked.

  2. Tap Enlarge to fill.

    Check: The row shows Enlarge to fill. From now on, the screens named above start with this mode.

  3. Tap the switch of Edge-to-edge video.

    Check: The switch is off. Full-screen video keeps clear of the Dynamic Island and the rounded corners. Tap the switch again to turn it on.

Hide offline cameras

The row has no section title. While the switch is on, cameras without a signal do not appear on the Live tab and on the Full Screen Wall. The device page still lists them, grayed out. The switch is off by default; the effect, the cameras that are hidden and the way hidden cameras return are described in “Hiding cameras without signal”.

  1. Tap the switch of Hide offline cameras.

    Check: The switch is on, and all cameras are checked again. Tap the switch again to turn it off.

  1. In the section LiveView Mix, tap Open on launch.

    Check: A menu lists the options. The current choice is marked.

  2. Tap the option that the app should show at its next start.

    Check: The row shows the choice. The app applies it once, each time it is started.

  3. Below the section LiveView Mix, tap the switch of Portrait page.

    Check: The switch is on. The section LiveView Mix now lists the rows Landscape page and Portrait page; the second shows Not set up until cameras or tiles are placed on it. Tap the switch again to turn the portrait page off; its content is kept.

Backup and sharing of cameras

Two sections move devices to another iPhone, iPad or Apple TV. A backup in iCloud serves the same Apple Account. A code serves any other device, with no iCloud, and can be given to another person.

RowWhat it doesDetails
Back Up to iCloudOpens iCloud Backup. A backup of the devices, their passwords and the app settings is stored in encrypted form in the private iCloud. Each backup to My Backup is kept as a separate backup and can be given a name; the Apple TV destination keeps one copy. A backup is made only when Back Up Now is tapped; nothing is backed up automatically.“Backing up to iCloud”, “Several named backups: renaming and deleting”
Restore from iCloudOpens Restore from iCloud, where the backup to restore is chosen. The devices of the backup are added to this device or updated, and the app settings of the backup are applied.“Restoring from iCloud”
Export CamerasOpens Export Cameras, which produces an encrypted code with the selected devices. The row is unavailable while no device exists.“Exporting cameras (creating a code)”
Import CamerasOpens Import Cameras, where a code is pasted to add its cameras.“Importing cameras”

The text below the sections reads Save your cameras and credentials, encrypted, to your private iCloud, then restore them on another iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV signed in to the same Apple Account. and Move your cameras to another iPhone, iPad, or someone else's Cam Hub as one encrypted code — no iCloud needed. The two ways are compared in “Comparing the two methods”.

Language

The section title and the row are always written in English, whatever language is selected, so that the row can still be found after switching to a language that is not readable. The default is Follow System, which uses the language setting of the iPhone or iPad.

  1. Tap the row Language.

    Check: A menu lists Follow System and the languages English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, العربية, Deutsch, Español, Español (México), Français and Slovenščina. Each language is written in its own name.

  2. Tap the language.

    Check: The whole app switches to the language at once. No restart is needed, and the screen stays on the Settings tab. For Arabic, the layout is mirrored from right to left; it returns to the usual direction when another language is selected.

The voice of the speedometer follows the language of the app; see “Speedometer and compass”.

Reset: defaults, delete all data, delete iCloud backup

The section Reset has three rows. Each one asks for a confirmation before it acts. The text below the section reads Restore Default Settings keeps your devices. Delete All Data also removes every device and saved password from this iPhone or iPad — your iCloud backup and subscription are kept.

Delete All DataEvery device with its channels, every saved password and SNMP setting, the downloaded recordings, the clips recorded in the single-camera view and the stored last pictures. Then everything that Restore Default Settings resets.iCloud backups and subscriptions.
Delete iCloud BackupEvery backup in iCloud: all backups of My Backup and the separate copy for Apple TV. The row is unavailable while no backup exists.Everything on this device.
Restore Default Settings also deletes every LiveView Mix page

The confirmation dialog states that the LiveView Mix wall is cleared, with its layouts, control panels, webhook buttons and pictures. This applies to every page: the landscape page, the portrait page and the two canvases of iPhone Duo, each with its selected cameras, its slots with clocks, labels and pictures, its control panels and its watermark. The pages are not devices and are not kept. They can be brought back only from an iCloud backup made earlier, or from a code that was exported with Include app settings; see “What backups and codes contain”. Restoring the default settings

  1. In the section Reset, tap Restore Default Settings.

    Check: The dialog Restore all settings to defaults? appears, with the buttons Restore Defaults and Cancel. The text of the dialog states that every setting, including the language, returns to its default, that the LiveView Mix wall (layouts, control panels, webhook buttons and pictures) is cleared, and that the devices and the iCloud backup are kept.

  2. Tap Restore Defaults. Tap Cancel instead to keep everything as it is.

    Check: The settings show their defaults, and the language follows the system. The row LiveView Mix shows Not set up, and the switch Portrait page is off. The devices are still listed in the Devices tab.

Deleting all data

Use this action to empty the app, for example before the device is passed on. It cannot be undone. Without an iCloud backup or an export code, the passwords of the cameras cannot be recovered, because this action removes them from this device.

  1. In the section Reset, tap Delete All Data.

    Check: The dialog Delete all data? appears, with the buttons Delete Everything and Cancel. Its text depends on the iCloud backup. With a backup, it reads This removes every device, camera, and saved password from this app. Your iCloud backup and subscription are not affected. Without a backup, it reads There's no iCloud backup, so this can't be undone — camera passwords live only on this device and can't be recovered. Your subscription is not affected.

  2. Tap Delete Everything. Tap Cancel instead to keep the data.

    Check: The Devices tab shows No Devices, and the settings show their defaults. An iCloud backup that exists can be restored as described in “Restoring from iCloud”.

Deleting the iCloud backup

On an iPhone or iPad the action removes every backup in iCloud: all backups of My Backup and the separate copy for Apple TV. A single backup is deleted from the backup list instead; see “Several named backups: renaming and deleting”. The steps and the details are in “Deleting all iCloud backups”.

  1. In the section Reset, tap Delete iCloud Backup. The row is unavailable while no backup exists.

    Check: The dialog Delete iCloud backup? appears. It states that all encrypted backups, including the Apple TV copy, are removed from iCloud permanently, that other devices signed in with the same Apple Account lose them when they next sync, and that the devices and settings on this device are not affected.

  2. Tap Delete iCloud Backup in the dialog.

    Check: The dialog closes, and the row Delete iCloud Backup becomes unavailable.

Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

The two rows in the section Legal open the pages of the website in the browser, outside the app. An internet connection is required. The pages are the same ones that are shown before a subscription is taken, and the same links are at the bottom of the plans page.

  1. In the section Legal, tap Terms of Use or Privacy Policy.

    Check: The browser opens the page https://cam-hub.almx.cc/terms.html or https://cam-hub.almx.cc/privacy.html. Return to Cam Hub with the app switcher of the device.

About, the user manual, and what Settings does not contain

  1. Scroll to the end of the Settings tab.

    Check: The section About has one row, Version. It shows the version number of the installed app, followed by a second number in parentheses. The row is for information and opens nothing. Below it is the row User Manual, with the text Step-by-step instructions for every feature, on the Cam Hub website.

  2. Tap User Manual.

    Check: The browser opens this manual at https://cam-hub.almx.cc/guide.html. An internet connection is required. Return to Cam Hub with the app switcher of the device.

  • Appearance: the app always uses a dark appearance. There is no setting for it.
  • Sound: sound is not switched in Settings but with the speaker button of the single-camera view; see “Snapshot, sound and quality”.
  • Names of devices and channels: they are changed on the device page, not in Settings; see “The device page”.
  • Disk Health and its SNMP settings: they are on the page of the device, not in Settings; see “Disk Health”.
  • Temperature unit: temperatures follow the unit of the region settings of the iPhone or iPad (Settings › General › Language & Region). Cam Hub has no setting of its own for it.
  • Notifications: Cam Hub shows no notifications and has no setting for them.
