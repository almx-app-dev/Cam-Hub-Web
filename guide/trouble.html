15Troubleshooting

This chapter lists, by symptom, what the empty screens, messages, permission requests and notices of Cam Hub mean, the likely cause and what to do. Connection failures while adding a device are covered in “When adding fails: the checklist” and are not repeated here.

Where to start

Most connection symptoms trace back to one of four causes. Check them in this order before reading the sections below.

  1. The iPhone or iPad is on the same Wi-Fi network as the device. A guest network, a separate VLAN, client isolation on the router and a VPN on the phone all block the connection.
  2. Cam Hub is allowed to use the local network; see “Permission requests and system dialogs”.
  3. The device is powered on and still has the address that was entered in Cam Hub. After a restart, a router can assign a different address to a device; a fixed address prevents this.
  4. The account and the password are still valid on the device. After several wrong attempts, a device can lock the account for a while.
SymptomWhere to look
A device cannot be added, or its connection indicator is red“When adding fails: the checklist”, “A device is added, but it is not reachable”
A screen is empty and shows only a title and a sentence“What the empty screens say”
A camera shows no picture, a black picture, an error message or no sound, or talking through it does not work“Live view”
The list of recordings is empty, the times differ, or playback does not speed up“Recording playback”
A tile of the LiveView Mix wall shows a status text, or the wall has changed“LiveView Mix”
A YouTube or Twitch tile shows a name and a sentence instead of the stream“YouTube and Twitch”

A device is added, but it is not reachable

The checklist after a failed attempt and the device report are described in “When adding fails: the checklist” and “Device report (experimental)”. The symptoms below appear after a device has been added.

A red, crossed-out Wi-Fi symbol in the device listThe device did not accept a connection on its HTTP port within about 4 seconds. It may be switched off, on another network, or blocked by a firewall. A device that was added with a brand chosen under Connect using, or one that uses another port for video, can show the red symbol while its video works.Check the power and the network. When the video plays, the symbol can be ignored. See “The device list”.

A device keeps the address with which it was added.Look up the new address on the router, delete the old device and add the device again with the new address; see “The Add Device form”. What is shownLikely causeWhat to do

The camera behind the channel is off, disconnected from the recorder, or has lost its network link.Check the power and the cable or network of the camera. When it is back, open the device page, or pull down on it, to read the channel list again. When a camera is missing from the Live tab altogether, check whether Hide offline cameras is on; see “Hiding cameras without signal”.
A message with a warning symbol and the button RetryThe stream could not be opened. The message names the cause, with the same wording as when a device is added.Find the sentence in “When adding fails: the checklist” or “Other error sentences”, correct the cause, then tap Retry.
Wrong username or password. for a device that worked beforeThe password was changed on the device, or the account was locked or removed. After one refusal, the other tiles of the same device fail at once without signing in, so that the device does not count a refused sign-in for every tile. The device is asked again after about a minute, or when the app returns to the foreground.Correct the account on the device, or wait until a lock ends, usually about 30 minutes. To store a new password, tap Edit Credentials on the device page; see “Changing the user name and password of a device”.
Connecting… for a long time, or Reconnecting… again and againThe network is weak, or the device does not answer. Cam Hub tries again at growing intervals of up to about 15 seconds, and after ten failed attempts in a row up to about a minute. A stream that delivers no picture for about 15 seconds is treated as interrupted and connected again, also when the camera still answers but its picture has frozen. A device can also serve only a limited number of viewers at the same time.Check the Wi-Fi signal. Close other viewers of the same camera. In a wall with many cameras, use SD instead of HD; see “SD and HD on device screens”.
A black picture without a messageThe camera sends a video format that the phone cannot decode. Cam Hub plays H.264 and H.265 only. An iPhone without hardware support for H.265, or a picture size above the limit of the decoder, shows no picture.In the stream settings of the camera, select H.264 and a lower resolution. A key frame interval of one to two seconds helps when the picture appears only after a long delay. See “Connection states and messages”.
The picture is blurred or blockyThe wall or the view uses SD, or the device offers only one stream. SD and HD are the same stream for some devices.Switch to HD in the single-camera view. Default values are set in the Settings tab; see “SD and HD on device screens”.
No speaker button, or no soundSound is off until the speaker button is tapped. The button appears only when the stream carries sound in a supported format (G.711 or AAC-LC). Sound plays only in the single-camera view and in the close-up of a wall, never on wall tiles and never in the background. Playback faster than 1× is muted. While someone talks through the camera, its own sound pauses.Tap the speaker button. When it is missing, the stream has no usable sound; turn on a supported audio format in the settings of the camera. For YouTube and Twitch, see “YouTube and Twitch”.
No talk button on the camera pageTalking is offered only in live view, for a camera that has a talk channel which Cam Hub can use, and not while the channel is beyond the plan. The channels of a recorder have none.See “Two-way talk”.
Microphone access is off for Cam Hub. Turn it on in Settings to talk through the camera.The microphone permission was refused.Turn the permission on in the system settings; see “Permission requests and system dialogs”.
This camera's talk channel is set to G.726. Set it to G.711 in the camera's audio settings to talk from Cam Hub. (the name of the format varies)The talk channel of the camera uses a sound format that Cam Hub does not send.In the audio settings of the camera, set the talk channel to G.711.
The microphone could not be started. Another app may be using it.Another app, or a call, holds the microphone.End the call or close the other app, then hold the talk button again.
Could not start talking to the camera. or The camera stopped taking sound.The camera did not open its talk channel, or closed it while someone was talking.Try again. Check that the account has the right to use the audio of the camera, and that no other app is talking through it.

Pictures stop when the app is left or the phone is lockedThis is intended. Every stream stops in the background, talking ends, and the streams connect again when the app returns.No action is needed. A recording that was playing resumes where it stopped.

Control panels and webhooks

What is shownLikely causeWhat to do

The screen does not show which one.Check, in this order: the address (it must start with http:// or https:// and name a host), the connection between the phone and the receiving side, and the log of the receiving side. Reaching the developer

On an iPhone or iPad, the last row of the Settings tab, Contact the Development Team, starts an email to the developer; see “Contacting the development team”. The row User Manual above it opens this manual. Apple TV has neither row. The developer can be reached in four ways:

  • Settings row: Contact the Development Team opens a new message in the email app of the device. The address, the subject, two questions and the version of the app are already filled in.
  • Support page: support.html on the website of Cam Hub. It holds notes on compatibility, a list of checks before a connection problem is reported, and the contact address.
  • Email: app-dev@almx.cc. The row Contact the Development Team shows this address, and the mail composer of the device report is addressed to it as well.
  • Device report: after a failed attempt to add a device, the experimental report collects technical details and offers to email them; see “Device report (experimental)”.

A message helps the developer most when it states:

  • the version of the app, shown in Settings › About › Version;
  • the model of the iPhone or iPad and the version of its system;
  • the brand and model of the camera or recorder, and the version of its firmware;
  • the connection method that was used;
  • the exact message that the screen showed, and what was tried before.

A message started with Contact the Development Team already names the version of the app, the system with its version, and whether the device is an iPhone or an iPad. It asks for the brand, model and firmware version of the camera or recorder, and for what does not work or which function is missing. The message can be written in any language.

Never send these items

The password of a camera, the PIN or password of a sharing code, a complete sharing code, an SNMP community, and the public address with which a device is reached over the internet. The developer never needs them.

A device that cannot be supported by design, for example one that works only through the cloud app of its maker, cannot be fixed by a message; see “Requirements and limits”. Questions about charges and refunds go to Apple.

Questions and answers

Why does the app always open on the LiveView Mix tab?

The app always opens on the first tab. In Settings › Open on launch, the Live tab of a chosen device, or the full-screen LiveView Mix wall, can open instead. The choice Last viewed does not restore the last tab. See “LiveView Mix in Settings”. Apple TV has no Open on launch; see “What differs on the Apple TV”.

Why is the app always dark?

Cam Hub has one appearance, and the settings contain no row for it.

Why are there no notifications for motion or alarms?

Cam Hub is a viewer. It sends no push notifications and no local notifications, and it asks for no permission for them.

Can cameras be watched from outside the home?

Cam Hub connects directly to the devices and uses no cloud service of its own. A device outside the home network can be reached only with an address that is reachable from the internet, or through a VPN back to the home network. How you reach a device from outside is your own choice.

Can a recording be saved or exported?

A part of a recording can. Play the recording, tap the red record button, and stop it when the moment has passed: the clip, up to 10 minutes long, can be sent or saved to the photo library. Whole recordings stay on the device, and Cam Hub cannot delete them; only a recording that arrives as a whole file can be shared whole, with the share button (Share Recording). A snapshot is a still image that the device supplies at the moment of the tap; during playback it shows the current live picture. See “Recording a clip”.

Why is there no sound?

Sound is off until the speaker button is tapped, and the button appears only when the stream carries sound in a supported format. Wall tiles, YouTube and Twitch tiles included, never play sound. See “Live view”.

Can Cam Hub talk through a camera?

Yes, for a camera that has a talk channel which Cam Hub can use: hold the talk button on the camera page and speak. See “Two-way talk”.

Why does SD look like HD?

Some devices serve one stream for every channel. SD and HD then show the same picture. Devices added with a brand chosen under Connect using usually behave this way. See “What works with which method”.

Why do the times of the recordings differ from the clock of the phone?

The times belong to the clock of the device and are never converted. Correct the date, the time and the time zone on the device; see “While a recording plays”.

A camera works in the app of its maker, but not in Cam Hub. What helps?

Try another connection method under Advanced › Connect using (ONVIF, RTSP or a brand from the list), switch on ONVIF and RTSP on the device, create a separate ONVIF user where the device has its own list, and check the video format. Devices that need the cloud of their maker cannot be added. See “Requirements and limits”. When none of this helps, or when the device has a function that Cam Hub does not offer yet, tell the development team with Contact the Development Team at the end of the Settings tab; see “Contacting the development team”.

Can a device that accepts only HTTPS be added?

Yes. Turn on Advanced › Use HTTPS in the add-device form; Cam Hub then adds the device over HTTPS and trusts the certificate that it presents. This applies to devices connected with Auto-detect or ONVIF. A device that has already been added is switched on its device page. See “Adding a device over HTTPS” and “HTTPS for a saved device”.

Why does Cam Hub ask for the passwords again after a move to a new iPhone, iPad or Mac?

The passwords did not arrive with the device list. Restore an iCloud backup of Cam Hub, which carries every password, or enter them again with Enter Password on each device page. See “A device without a saved password”.

How are the address or the password of a saved device changed?

The username and the password are changed with Edit Credentials on the device page; see “Changing the user name and password of a device”. The address cannot be changed: a new address creates a new device, so delete the old one first. See “The Add Device form”.

How many cameras can be watched without a plan?

Two enabled channels in the whole app, and one recorder. YouTube live streams and Twitch channels do not count. See “The allowance of each plan”.

Does a plan apply to all devices that use the same Apple Account?

Yes, on every iPhone, iPad and Apple TV that is signed in with the same Apple Account. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases. See “Subscribing, restoring purchases and redeeming a code”.

All data was deleted. Can the cameras be recovered?

Only from an iCloud backup that was made before, with Restore from iCloud (see “Restoring from iCloud”), or from a sharing code that was exported before (see “Importing cameras”). Without either, the passwords of the cameras cannot be recovered, because the action removed them from this device. Plans are not affected by Delete All Data.

Does the device report send the password?

No. The password is never part of the report, and nothing is sent unless the person chooses to email it. See “Device report (experimental)”.

Why does the screen not turn off?

While a live picture or a recording is on the screen, the app keeps the screen awake. Leaving the video, or leaving the app, ends this. On Apple TV, the app keeps the Apple TV from going to sleep for as long as Cam Hub is the app in front, whatever screen it shows, so that the sleep setting of the Apple TV does not end a wall. Leaving the app ends this.

Why does the picture stop when another app is opened?

Every stream stops when the app leaves the foreground, and sound never plays in the background. When the app returns, the streams connect again.
