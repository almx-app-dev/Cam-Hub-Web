11Plans and limits

Cam Hub comes with a free basic allowance. When more channels or recorders are needed at the same time, subscription plans extend it. All plans offer the same functions; they differ only in the number of camera channels and recorders that can be enabled at the same time. YouTube and Twitch streams are outside every plan and always free.

The allowance of each plan

PlanBillingWhat can be used at the same time
FreeNoneAt most 2 enabled channels in the whole app, counting the channels of standalone cameras and of recorders together. At most 1 recorder, and its enabled channels must all be on that one recorder.
Cam Home — 8 camerasYearlyUp to 8 standalone cameras can be enabled.
Cam Plus — 16 camerasYearlyUp to 16 standalone cameras can be enabled.
Cam Max — unlimited camerasYearlyAny number of standalone cameras can be enabled.
NVR Starter — 2 recordersMonthly2 recorders can be connected, and every channel of these 2 can be used.
NVR Pro — 4 recordersMonthly4 recorders can be connected, and every channel of these 4 can be used.
NVR Max — unlimited recordersMonthlyAny number of recorders can be connected, and every channel can be used.

Camera plans limit the number of enabled channels of standalone cameras, which are cameras that are not behind a recorder. NVR plans count recorders, not channels: every channel of a recorder that the plan covers is unlocked. The cost of each plan is shown on the plans screen of the app.

How the two groups of plans combine

Camera plans and NVR plans are two independent groups of subscriptions. Both can be held at the same time, and each decides the allowance of its own side.

Plans heldStandalone cameras (enabled channels)Recorders
None (free)At most 2, counted together with the channels of recorders1 recorder; its enabled channels must be on that one recorder, and together with standalone cameras the total stays at 2
A camera plan onlyAccording to the plan: 8, 16 or unlimitedStill the free allowance: 1 recorder with at most 2 enabled channels, no longer counted together with standalone cameras
An NVR plan onlyStill the free allowance: at most 2According to the plan: 2, 4 or unlimited recorders, with every channel of these recorders usable
BothAccording to the camera planAccording to the NVR plan
  • The two sides are counted separately. Unlocking recorders does not raise the limit for standalone cameras, and a higher camera plan does not add recorders.
  • Within one group only one plan is active at a time. A higher plan replaces a lower plan; they do not add up.
  • When both groups are raised to unlimited, Cam Hub turns on every channel once, at that moment, and the row Choose which cameras to show is hidden because nothing is left to choose. A channel that is disabled afterwards stays disabled.

What uses allowance

ItemHow it is counted
Enabled channels of standalone camerasEach channel uses 1 camera allowance.
RecordersCounted by number. While a recorder is within the number that the plan covers, every channel of it can be used. Without an NVR plan, the free allowance applies: at most 2 enabled channels on 1 recorder.
Disabled channelsThey use no allowance and do not stream.
YouTube and Twitch streamsThey use no allowance on any plan, including the free plan, and are never locked. A stream is switched on when it is added and can always be switched on again. See “YouTube and Twitch streams”.
Control panelsEach control panel uses 1 camera allowance, the same as a standalone camera, but only from what the standalone cameras leave free: a camera can always be switched on in the place of a panel, and the panel then locks. An NVR plan alone keeps the camera allowance at 2, but recorder channels no longer take from it, so control panels use what the standalone cameras leave of these 2. Clock, label, picture and empty tiles use no allowance. See Chapter 9, “Control panels and webhooks”.
Control panels on several LiveView Mix pagesEach panel on each page in use counts on its own: the landscape page always; the portrait page while Portrait page is on (iPhone and iPad); the lower canvas on an iPhone Duo while Two canvases is on. A panel copied to another page counts again. On Apple TV only the panels of the landscape page count. See “The portrait page”.
Tiles of LiveView MixThe number of tiles of a wall is not limited; what is limited is the channels that can stream. LiveView Mix cannot show more cameras than the allowance of the plan.

When the allowance is not sufficient, items keep their picture in this order: first standalone cameras (in the order in which their devices were added, then by channel number), then the channels of recorders, and last control panels (those of the landscape page first, then those of the lower canvas, then those of the portrait page). On the free plan, only the first 2 of all these together are kept, so a control panel never displaces a camera. With a camera plan, control panels receive what the standalone cameras leave of the camera allowance. With an NVR plan alone, they receive what the standalone cameras leave of the 2 free camera allowances, whatever the number of recorder channels. With an NVR plan, the first recorders in sequence keep all their channels, and the channels of the remaining recorders are locked.

A control panel beyond the allowance keeps its place on the wall and shows a lock, its title and Not in your plan; its buttons are unavailable.

When a recorder is added and the number allowed by the plan has been reached, the plans screen opens with the text Free includes 1 recorder. Add more with an NVR plan. and the recorder is not added. While the demo devices hold the free recorder, the text reads Free includes 1 recorder, and the demo devices are using it. Delete them to add your own recorder, or add more with an NVR plan.; see “Trying the app without a device”.

Choosing which cameras show

Path: Settings › Choose which cameras to show. When the allowance of the plan is smaller than the total number of channels of the devices, the user decides which channels use it. This page, titled like the row, gathers the channels of all devices on one screen, with one switch per channel; for the steps, see “Choosing which cameras show”.

  • What it does: a switch controls whether a channel is enabled. A channel that is off does not stream and uses no allowance, and it can be turned on again at any time; a channel that is on uses allowance. The switches on this page are the same function as Enable and Disable after touching and holding a channel tile on the device page; see “Channel options (long press)”. YouTube and Twitch streams are listed as well; their switches never touch the allowance.
  • The Your plan section shows the limits of the current plan: Cameras is the number of channels of standalone cameras that can be enabled, and Recorders is the number of recorders whose channels can all be used; each is a number or Unlimited.
  • When the allowance is used up, turning a switch on is refused: the switch stays off, and the plans screen opens with the reason at the top, for example You've reached the Free plan's limit of 2 enabled cameras., You've reached your plan's limit of 8 enabled cameras., On the Free plan, the cameras you show must all be on one recorder. or Unlock every channel with an NVR plan. Turning a switch off is always possible.
  • When it is offered: the item is available only while at least one of the two limits of the plan is in effect. When both groups of plans are raised to unlimited, every channel is turned on automatically and the item is hidden. While no device exists, the page shows No Devices with Add a device first, then choose its cameras here. and the Add Device button.
  • To obtain more allowance, subscribe as described in “Subscribing, restoring purchases and redeeming a code”. Channels beyond the allowance show Not in your plan and a lock on the wall and on the device page; see “What a thumbnail shows”.
  • On Apple TV, the row in Settings opens a page titled License, with the same Your plan section and a switch for each channel, grouped by device. A switch that the allowance does not permit stays off, and an alert states the same reason; OK closes it, and the plans screen does not open. Without devices, the page shows No Devices without the Add Device button. See “The Settings tab”.

Subscribing, restoring purchases and redeeming a code

Prerequisite: this device is signed in with an Apple Account in the system settings, and purchases are permitted. A subscription follows the Apple Account and is shared across iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

  1. Tap the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen, then tap Plans in the Subscription section.

    Check: The Plans page opens. Below the row Plans, the current plan is shown; without a subscription it reads Free.

  2. In the section Camera plans · billed yearly or NVR plans · billed monthly, find the plan to take and tap Subscribe on its card.

    Check: The system purchase confirmation appears. Each card shows the name of the plan, a short description and its cost; when the Apple Account is eligible, the length of a free trial is shown as well.

  3. Follow the prompts of the system screen to confirm the purchase with the Apple Account.

    Check: The card of the plan changes to Current Plan, and the allowance is updated immediately. The row Plans in the Settings tab shows the name of the plan.

  4. Tap Done in the top-right corner.

    Check: The plans page closes and the settings list is shown again.

  • A subscription renews automatically until the user cancels it. Camera plans are billed yearly, NVR plans monthly.
  • The outcome of a purchase that does not complete is shown below the card of the plan: Purchase failed. Please try again.; Waiting for approval. The plan unlocks as soon as the purchase is approved. when the purchase needs approval, for example by a family organizer; or The App Store could not confirm this purchase. Try Restore Purchases.
  • The plan cards appear once the purchase service has answered; until then, a spinner is shown below the section titles. When the plans cannot be loaded, the page shows Couldn't load plans. Check your connection and try again. instead of the spinner. Closing the page and opening it again loads the plans once more.
  • Manage Subscription opens the system subscription page, where a plan can be canceled or changed and the renewal date can be seen.
  • While plans of both groups are held, the row Plans joins the names of the two plans with a plus sign.
  • The text at the top of the plans page states that every plan includes all functions, including live view, recording playback and the multi-camera wall; that video does not pass through a cloud and needs no account; and that the two limits are counted separately. At the bottom of the page are the links Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Subscribing on Apple TV

  1. In the Settings tab of the Apple TV, select Plans.

    Check: The Plans page opens. The Subscriptions section begins with the row Current plan, which names the current plan (Free without a subscription), followed by one row for each camera plan and each NVR plan with its picture, name and short description and, at the right, its cost with the billing period. When the Apple Account is eligible for a free trial, its length is shown in green below the description.

  2. Select the row of the plan to take.

    Check: A spinner replaces the cost, and the system purchase confirmation appears.

  3. Follow the prompts of the system screen to confirm the purchase with the Apple Account.

    Check: The row of the plan shows a checkmark in place of the cost, the row Current plan names the plan, and the allowance is updated immediately.

  • A row cannot be selected while its cost has not been loaded, while it is the current plan, or while another purchase is running.
  • Until the purchase service answers, each row shows Billed yearly or Billed monthly in place of the cost, and an unavailable row at the end of the section reports that the costs are loading. When they cannot be loaded, that row turns into a button for trying again; select it to load them once more.
  • The outcome of a purchase that does not complete appears in an alert: Purchase failed. Please try again., Waiting for approval. The plan unlocks as soon as the purchase is approved. or The App Store could not confirm this purchase. Try Restore Purchases. Select OK to close it.
  • The note below the plans states that the two limits are counted separately, and that the subscription follows the Apple Account: a plan can be taken on the Apple TV or on an iPhone or iPad and then synchronized with Restore Purchases. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled.
  • The Legal section follows, with Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy, which show their texts on the television, and then the Restore Purchases button, which shows a spinner while it runs. On Apple TV, this is the only place with Restore Purchases.

Restoring purchases

Use this on another device, after a reinstallation, or when a purchase is not reflected immediately.

  1. In the Subscription section of the Settings tab, tap Restore Purchases. The same button is at the bottom of the plans page. On Apple TV, select Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Plans page.

    Check: The app synchronizes the purchase records with the system purchase service and shows a message.

  2. Read the message and dismiss it.

    Check: When purchases are found, the message reads Purchases restored. and the allowance is updated. When none are found, it reads No purchases to restore. After an error it reads Restore failed. Please try again. When the system sign-in screen is canceled, no message appears.

Redeeming a code

  1. In the Subscription section of the Settings tab, tap Redeem Code. The same button is at the bottom of the plans page.

    Check: The system screen for redeeming a code appears. When it cannot be opened, the alert Couldn't open code redemption. Check your connection, or redeem the code in the App Store. appears instead.

  2. Follow the prompts of the system screen to enter and redeem the code.

    Check: After a successful redemption, the plan and the allowance are updated. When they are not updated yet, synchronize with Restore Purchases.

On Apple TV, Manage Subscription and Redeem Code are not offered; they are used on an iPhone or an iPad. See “The Settings tab”.

When a plan ends or is reduced

  • Cam Hub checks the plan whenever the app returns to the foreground. When a subscription ends without renewal, or a lower plan takes its place, the allowance shrinks accordingly.
  • When the App Store cannot confirm the purchases, for example while the device is offline or signed out of the App Store, the last confirmed plan stays in effect for up to 14 days. A plan that Apple reports as ended, refunded or replaced by another plan of the same group ends at once.
  • The enabled state of channels is not rewritten, and the devices, names, wall layouts and settings are all kept. Channels beyond the new allowance stop streaming, and their tiles show Not in your plan and a lock. Tapping such a tile opens the plans screen with the reason. A locked channel cannot be used for recording playback either.
  • The order in which items are kept is the one described in “What uses allowance”: standalone cameras first, then the channels of recorders, and last control panels. A locked control panel shows a lock, its title and Not in your plan, and its buttons are unavailable. YouTube and Twitch streams are never locked.
  • After subscribing again or restoring purchases, the locked channels stream again, and no device has to be added again.
  • When a backup is restored or a code is imported while a plan is held, channels beyond its allowance are written as Disabled; such channels must be turned on again, as described in “Choosing which cameras show”, once the allowance is sufficient. On the free plan the enabled state is kept, and the channels beyond the free allowance stay locked until a plan covers them. See Chapter 10, “Sharing and backup”.
