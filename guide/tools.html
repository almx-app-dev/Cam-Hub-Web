12The Tools tab

The Tools tab contains the network scan for finding devices, the speedometer with compass, and a section that adds sample devices. The scan is described step by step in the chapter on adding a device; this chapter gives an overview of the tab and describes the speedometer.

What the Tools tab contains

Path: Tools tab at the bottom of the screen. The tab is a single list. The sections follow each other in the order below.

SectionContentsDetails
SubnetThe row This device with the own address of the iPhone or iPad, the address field of the range, the selector Range, the line that states what will be scanned, the button Use my subnet (only while the range differs from the own network) and the button Scan or Stop.“Finding devices with the network scan”
Found DevicesThe progress of the scan, the addresses that answered with their open ports and, once the sweep is over, what the devices say about themselves: a name or a model and, for a recorder, the number of channels. A tap on a result opens the Add Device form with the address, and what the device said about itself, filled in.“Finding devices with the network scan”
SpeedometerThe row Speedometer, which opens the speedometer with compass. The section is offered depending on the region and the language setting of the device.“Speedometer and compass”
DemoThe button Add Demo Devices (App Review), which adds sample devices.Below on this page.

  1. Tap the Tools tab at the bottom of the screen.

    Check: The screen shows the sections of the tab, beginning with Subnet. The address field and the range are already filled in with the network of the device.

For the steps of a scan, see “Finding devices with the network scan”. The following points concern the behavior of the tab as a whole.

  • Stored range: the address and the range are stored. When the device joins another network, both fields are filled in again for the new network, unless the range was typed by hand; a range that was typed by hand is kept. The button Use my subnet hands the choice back to the app. Restore Default Settings forgets the stored range; see “Reset: defaults, delete all data, delete iCloud backup”.
  • While a scan runs: the address field and the range cannot be changed. Stop ends the scan. A running scan also ends when the screen is left.
  • No sign-in: the scan checks only whether connections to the ports can be made and reads the name that a device announces on its web port. When the sweep is over, the list shows Identifying devices… while the app asks the devices it found, through the discovery services of the local network, to name themselves. It does not sign in to any device. A device is added only after the credentials have been entered in the Add Device form.
  • Identified devices: when a result was opened for a device that stated its brand, and the address was not changed, the Add Device form leaves out the compatibility reference and Add Demo Cameras, which only help with an unknown device.
  • Local network access: the first scan can cause the system to ask whether Cam Hub may find devices on the local network. When the access is refused, the scan finds nothing. After a complete scan without results, the list adds the sentence If your cameras are on this network, check that Cam Hub is allowed to find devices on your local network in the system privacy settings.; see “Finding devices with the network scan” and “Permission requests and system dialogs”.

Adding sample devices

The section Demo is at the end of the tab. Its text states that the devices are meant for the review of the app and that everyday use does not need them. They can be used by anyone to try the app without equipment. The sample devices draw their own pictures and need no network and no sign-in.

  1. Scroll to the end of the Tools tab and tap Add Demo Devices (App Review).

    Check: The app adds five recorders and two cameras. Each recorder has three channels. The recorders are named Demo NVR 1 to Demo NVR 5, and the cameras Demo Camera 1 and Demo Camera 2.

  2. Tap the Devices tab.

    Check: The list shows the sample devices. Their channels are enabled only as far as the current plan allows; the other channels show Disabled; see “Plan limits and enabling channels”.

  • Added once: after a tap, the button is dimmed. When the sample devices already exist, it adds nothing.
  • Counted like real devices: the sample recorders count toward the number of recorders that the plan allows, and enabled sample channels use places of the plan. Delete the sample devices before a real recorder is added on the free plan; see “The device list”.
  • Follow the plan: the app sets the enabled state of the sample channels again whenever it starts and whenever the plan changes, so that they always show what the plan allows. A switch that was changed by hand on a sample channel is not kept.
  • Limited functions: sample devices have no recording playback, no camera control and no snapshot, and the device page cannot refresh their channel list.

Speedometer and compass

The speedometer shows the speed of the device and the direction in which it moves or points. The position comes from satellite positioning. Whether the Tools tab offers the speedometer depends on the region and the language setting of the device. When it is not offered, the Tools tab shows the other sections only.

Opening the speedometer and allowing location access

Prerequisite: the iPhone or iPad has a clear view of the sky. Indoors, the position can be delayed or missing.

  1. Tap the Tools tab, scroll to the section Speedometer and tap the row Speedometer.

    Check: The screen Speedometer opens. When Cam Hub has not been allowed to use the location before, a system dialog asks for permission.

  2. Tap Allow While Using App.

    Check: The screen shows Waiting for GPS… until the first position has been received. The message then disappears.

  3. Move with the device, for example in a vehicle.

    Check: The large number shows the speed, and the compass dial turns with the direction.

Reading the screen

ElementMeaning
Large numberThe speed in km/h, rounded to whole numbers. The number shows — until the first measurement. A device at rest shows 0.
km/h and the line with mphThe same speed in both units. Both are always shown. There is no setting for the unit. Before the first measurement, the line reads 0 mph.
Compass dialThe dial turns below a fixed pointer at the top. The direction in which the device moves or points is the one below the pointer. The ring has a tick every 5 degrees and the letters N, E, S and W.
Text in the middle of the dialThe name of the direction (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W or NW) and the heading in degrees, counted clockwise from north. The text shows – until a heading is available.

The heading comes from two sources. While the device moves at 8 km/h or faster, it is the direction of travel determined by satellite positioning. At lower speeds, it is the heading of the compass of the device. When no compass reading exists, the last direction of travel is shown.

Messages below the compass

MessageMeaning and action
Waiting for GPS… (grey)No position has been received yet. Wait a few seconds, or move to a place with a clear view of the sky.
Can't get a GPS fix. (orange)The position could not be determined. The message disappears as soon as a position arrives. When it remains, move to a place with a clear view of the sky, or leave the screen and open it again.
Location access is off. Turn it on in Settings to see your speed and heading. (orange), with the button Open SettingsCam Hub is not allowed to use the location. Allow it as described below.
No messageA position is known. Speed and heading are current.

Allowing location access afterwards

  1. Tap Open Settings below the orange message.

    Check: The system settings of Cam Hub open.

  2. Tap Location and select While Using the App.

    Check: The entry Location shows While Using the App.

  3. Return to Cam Hub, tap the back button of the speedometer and open the row Speedometer again.

    Check: The screen shows Waiting for GPS… and then the speed and the heading.

Announcing the speed by voice

The switch Announce speed at the bottom of the screen is on by default. Its text reads Says your speed aloud every 10 km/h, from 150 up to 450 km/h.

  1. Tap the row Announce speed.

    Check: The switch is off. No speed is announced. The choice is stored.

  2. Tap the row Announce speed again.

    Check: The switch is on. The voice announces speeds from now on.

  • What is said: only the number in km/h, without a unit. The voice speaks once each time the speed passes the next multiple of ten, from 150 up to 450. Nothing is said below 150 or while the speed falls.
  • No repetition: a number is not repeated while the speed fluctuates slightly around it. It is said again only after the speed has fallen clearly below it and then risen again.
  • Switching on at speed: when the voice is switched on at a high speed, numbers that have already been passed are not announced. The next multiple of ten is the first one.
  • Language of the voice: the voice follows the language of the app (Settings › Language). When the device has no voice for that language, the English voice is used.
  • Other audio: music or other audio that plays at the same time becomes quieter while a number is spoken and continues afterwards.
  • Backup and reset: the switch is not part of the backups, and Restore Default Settings does not change it.

Leaving the speedometer

  1. Tap the back button in the top-left corner.

    Check: The Tools tab is shown again. The use of the location and the announcements stop.

  • Screen stays on: while the speedometer is open, the device does not lock the screen automatically.
  • Location only while open: Cam Hub uses the location only while the screen is open, and only while the app is in use. The position is used for the display and is not stored.
