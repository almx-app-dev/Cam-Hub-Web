14Apple TV

The Apple TV app shows the devices and the LiveView Mix wall that were set up on an iPhone or an iPad. The recommended order is to finish the whole setup on an iPhone or iPad first and to bring it to the Apple TV afterwards. This chapter gives the steps for that and lists what differs on the television. It is not an operating guide for the Apple TV app.

Set up on an iPhone or iPad first

The Apple TV is operated with a remote. Typing, drawing and choosing are laborious there, and several functions are not offered at all. These tasks are therefore done on an iPhone or an iPad before the Apple TV is used:

  • Adding the devices; see Chapter 3, “Adding a device”.
  • Building the LiveView Mix wall, with a custom layout and control panels where wanted; see Chapter 8, “LiveView Mix” and Chapter 9, “Control panels and webhooks”.
  • Choosing the plans and the cameras that show; see Chapter 11, “Plans and limits”.

Bringing the setup to the Apple TV

Prerequisites: the iPhone or iPad and the Apple TV are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, and both run the same version of Cam Hub. A backup that an older version made does not appear on the Apple TV.

  1. On the iPhone or iPad, finish the setup: the devices, the LiveView Mix wall, the plans and the choice of cameras that show.

    Check: The devices and the wall appear on the iPhone or iPad the way they are to appear on the television.

  2. On the iPhone or iPad, back up to iCloud with Apple TV as the destination; see “Backing up to iCloud”.

    Check: The backup page shows the message that the backup was made, and Current Backup shows the time and the number of devices. A new backup to Apple TV replaces the previous Apple TV copy.

  3. On the Apple TV, open the Settings tab and select Restore from iCloud in the iCloud Backup section.

    Check: The page shows the time of the backup in From and its contents in Devices. When the section shows No backup found in iCloud yet instead of the row, see “Sharing, import and iCloud backup”.

  4. Select Restore. What a restore changes is described in “Restoring from iCloud”.

    Check: The page shows how many devices were restored. The Devices tab of the Apple TV lists them, and the LiveView Mix tab shows the wall.

  5. On the Apple TV, select Plans in the Settings tab.

    Check: The page shows the plan of the Apple Account. A plan that is not shown yet appears after Restore Purchases.

The Apple TV restores only the copy that was backed up for it. The named backups kept under My Backup are listed only on the iPhone and the iPad; see “Several named backups: renaming and deleting”.

When the two devices do not share an Apple Account, a sharing code replaces the backup. Create the code on the iPhone or iPad as described in “Exporting cameras (creating a code)” and copy it. On the Apple TV, select Share Cameras › Import Cameras in the Settings tab and enter the code; when the keyboard appears, a nearby iPhone can type it. A locked code asks for its PIN or password. A code can be hundreds to thousands of characters long, so an iCloud backup is preferable whenever the Apple Account is the same.

Later changes reach the Apple TV only with a new backup

After devices, the wall or the plans have been changed on the iPhone or iPad, repeat steps 2 to 4. The Apple TV does not follow changes by itself. A changed password of a device can also be entered on the Apple TV with Edit Credentials on the device page.
