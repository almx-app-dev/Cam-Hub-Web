5The Live tab and the Full Screen Wall

The Live tab shows the enabled channels of one device as a wall of live pictures. On this tab the quality of the wall is switched, single cameras are opened, and the wall is extended to the Full Screen Wall. Cameras without a signal can be hidden from both walls. YouTube and Twitch streams appear on the walls like cameras.

The Live tab

Path: Live tab at the bottom of the screen.

The tab shows one device at a time: a recorder with all of its enabled channels, or one standalone camera. The title of the screen is the name of that device. To show cameras of different devices on one wall, use LiveView Mix; see Chapter 8, “LiveView Mix”.

  • Enabled channels only: channels that are disabled never appear on this tab. They are enabled on the device page; see “Channel options (long press)”.
  • Silent tiles: the tiles of a wall play no sound, YouTube and Twitch tiles included. Sound is available in the single-camera view and in the full-screen viewer of a stream; see “Snapshot, sound and quality”.
  • YouTube and Twitch: a stream that was added as a device is shown as one tile with the player of the service. A tap on the tile opens the stream full screen; see “YouTube and Twitch streams”.
  • No refresh on this tab: the channel list of a device is read from the device when its device page opens; see “The device page”.
  • Leaving the app: when the app leaves the foreground, all streams stop. When the app returns, the tiles connect again.

Opening the Live tab

  1. Tap the Live tab at the bottom of the screen.

    Check: The screen shows the live pictures of the enabled channels of one device. The title is the name of the device. As long as no other device has been selected, the first device of the Devices tab is shown.

ControlPositionFunction

SD or HDTop right, firstSwitches the quality of the whole wall at once (Switch all cameras to HD or Switch all cameras to SD). The text on the button is the current quality. See “SD and HD on a wall”. Not shown for a YouTube or Twitch stream.

While no device has been added, the SD/HD button and the Full Screen Wall button are unavailable. The list button (Select Device) is unavailable as well, unless the LiveView Mix wall still holds a picture, a clock, a label or a control panel; in that case the button can be tapped, but its menu is empty. The black-bar button starts with the mode of Settings › Picture › Black bars. The Live tab reads that mode when the app starts, so a change in Settings reaches the Live tab at the next start of the app.

Showing another device

  1. Tap the list button (Select Device) in the top-left corner.

    Check: A menu lists all devices in the order of the Devices tab. The device that is shown is marked.

  2. Tap the name of the device.

    Check: The wall shows the enabled channels of the selected device, and the title changes to its name. The choice is stored and is still valid after the app has been closed.

The device for the Live tab can also be chosen in Open on launch; see “LiveView Mix in Settings”. When the selected device is deleted, the wall shows the first device of the list.

Opening a camera

  1. Tap a tile that shows a live picture.

    Check: The single-camera view opens with the name of the camera as its title; see Chapter 6, “Single-camera view”. The back button returns to the Live tab. A tile of a YouTube or Twitch stream opens the stream full screen instead.

  2. Tap a tile that shows Not in your plan.

    Check: The plans screen opens with a message at the top: Unlock every channel with an NVR plan. for a channel of a recorder, You've reached the Free plan's limit of 2 enabled cameras. for a standalone camera on the Free plan, and You've reached your plan's limit of 8 enabled cameras. for a standalone camera while a plan is held; the number is the camera limit of that plan. Close the screen with Done.

When the tab is empty

  • No device has been added: the screen shows No Devices and Add a device in the Devices tab first. See Chapter 3, “Adding a device”.
  • The device has no enabled channel, or every channel is hidden by Hide offline cameras: the screen shows No Enabled Cameras and Enable cameras on the device page. See “Choosing which cameras show”.

SD and HD on a wall

SD is the lighter picture and HD the sharper one. The button at the top of the Live tab and the button on the Full Screen Wall switch all cameras of the wall together.

  1. Tap the quality button at the top right of the Live tab (SD or HD).

    Check: The text on the button changes to the other quality, and the tiles connect again with the new quality.

  2. Tap the button again.

    Check: The wall returns to the first quality.

  • Starting quality: Settings › Default quality › Multiple cameras (SD unless changed). A change in Settings reaches the Live tab at once, until the button has been tapped on the Live tab; from then on the wall keeps that choice until the app is closed. The Full Screen Wall starts with the setting every time it opens.
  • Not stored: a quality chosen with the button is never saved.
  • One stream per channel: some devices provide only one stream for a channel. For those, SD and HD show the same picture.
  • Close-up: the close-up of a camera in the Full Screen Wall uses Single camera and has no switch; see “The full-screen wall of a device”.
  • For the quality on all other screens, see “SD and HD on device screens”; for the settings, see “Default quality”.

The Full Screen Wall

The Full Screen Wall shows all enabled channels of the device edge to edge on a black background. The status bar is hidden, and the picture reaches the edges of the screen as set by Edge-to-edge video; see “Picture: black bars and edge-to-edge”. The wall opens from the Live tab and from the device page; for the device page, see “The full-screen wall of a device”.

Opening the wall

  1. Tap the Full Screen Wall button (diagonal arrows) at the top right of the Live tab.

    Check: The wall covers the screen. At the top, the close button is on the left and the buttons for the quality, the layout and the black bars are on the right. The buttons are round or capsule-shaped with a white ring, so that they stay visible on any picture. For a YouTube or Twitch stream, only the close button and the layout button are shown. When the device has no enabled channel, the screen shows No Enabled Cameras.

Changing the number of tiles per row

  1. Tap Layout at the top right of the wall.

    Check: The tiles are arranged one per row or two per row, and the symbol of the button changes. The next time the wall opens, it uses the same arrangement.

Opening a camera and closing the wall

  1. Tap a tile that shows a live picture.

    Check: A close-up of the camera opens over the wall, with Back and the name of the camera at the top. Its zoom, speaker, camera control and recordings are described in “The full-screen wall of a device”. The other cameras of the wall pause while the close-up is open. A tile with Not in your plan opens the plans screen, a tile with No signal does nothing, and a tile of a YouTube or Twitch stream opens the stream full screen (see “YouTube and Twitch streams”).

  2. Tap Close (the cross at the top left of the wall).

    Check: The Live tab is shown again.

Hiding cameras without signal

The setting Hide offline cameras removes cameras without a signal from the Live tab and from the Full Screen Wall. It is off by default. The device page still lists every channel and shows the channels without a signal in grey; see “What a thumbnail shows”. LiveView Mix walls are not affected: a slot without a signal keeps a plate with No signal. This section describes iPhone and iPad. On Apple TV, the setting also applies to the device page and to the camera preview of the LiveView Mix tab; see “The Settings tab”.

  1. Tap the Settings tab, then scroll to Hide offline cameras. The row is in its own section below Picture.

    Check: The switch is off. The text below the row reads Cameras with no signal are hidden from the live walls. The device page still lists them, grayed out.

  2. Turn on Hide offline cameras.

    Check: The switch is on. All cameras are checked again.

  3. Tap the Live tab.

    Check: Cameras without a signal no longer appear on the wall. When no camera of the device remains, the screen shows No Enabled Cameras.

  • Which cameras are hidden: channels that the device reports as having no signal, and cameras whose stream could not be opened twice in a row. Cam Hub tries once more before a camera is hidden.
  • When a hidden camera returns: a hidden camera is not tried again until the app returns to the foreground, until the switch is turned off and on again, or until new credentials or a changed HTTPS setting are saved for its device (see “Changing the user name and password of a device” and “HTTPS for a saved device”). The camera is then tried once more: it stays on the wall when it shows a picture, and it is hidden again when it fails again. A channel that the device reports as having no signal returns when the device reports a signal again; the report is updated when the device page reads the channel list.
  • Where it applies: to the Live tab and to the Full Screen Wall, including the Full Screen Wall of the device page. The setting is part of the backup of the app settings and is reset by Restore Default Settings; see “Reset: defaults, delete all data, delete iCloud backup”.
